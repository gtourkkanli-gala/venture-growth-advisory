How we work

Challenge, clarity, then a decision.

01  Challenge

Assumptions get tested before the market tests them.

Expect direct questions and straight answers. The aim is not to do your job for you. It is to find the gaps while they are still cheap to fix.

02  Clarity

Gaps, priorities and trade-offs in plain words.

Written so the whole team, and later an investor, sees the same picture. No consulting language where a plain sentence will do.

03  Execution

Every session ends with decisions and next steps.

Things you can act on this month, not a report for the drawer. The business stays yours; the judgement is shared.

Fit

A good fit, or not.

Likely a good fit

  • +You have a real business or technology idea and want it tested properly.
  • +You are preparing for pre-seed, seed or Series A and want the gaps found first.
  • +You run a programme and want founders challenged, not flattered.
  • +You want decisions and next steps, not a report for the drawer.

Probably not

  • −You want someone to write the plan or the deck for you.
  • −You want guaranteed introductions or a success fee on money raised.
  • −You want reassurance rather than questions.
  • −You are looking for general business coaching rather than a specific commercial challenge.
