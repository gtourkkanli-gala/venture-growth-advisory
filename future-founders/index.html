Future Founders

Help young people see themselves as builders, not only consumers.

We dedicate selected time to pro bono entrepreneurship support for children and young adults: making business, technology and startup thinking practical, understandable and possible.

Why it matters

Commercial work should create room for contribution.

We want young people to understand that businesses are created by ordinary people who learn how to solve problems, test ideas, build value and keep going. We work selectively with schools, community organisations and young people where we can make that journey tangible.

Pro bono support is selective and capacity-dependent. Schools, community organisations and young people can contact us with a short description of the opportunity.
