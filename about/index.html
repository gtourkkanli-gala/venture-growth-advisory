Gokhan is a founder, business adviser, angel investor and programme leader with experience across startup support, commercialisation, investor readiness, programme delivery and business strategy. He holds an MBA and is completing a DBA at Golden Gate University — San Francisco & Silicon Valley, with doctoral work focused on improving early-stage fundraising and investment decision-making. He is also building Investra AI, an AI platform focused on more explainable, evidence-linked capital decisions.

How the work is approached

The starting point is the business problem, not a pre-packaged methodology. Advice is expected to survive contact with customers, investors, partners, teams and cash constraints. That means clear assumptions, evidence where possible, explicit trade-offs and a short path from strategy to action.
